02 — My Story

Why Product.
Why Now.

I spent 4 years being the last person who touched a product before a real user did. Every bug I caught was a product decision made without enough user empathy. Every edge case I documented was a gap in the original thinking.

Over time, I stopped asking "does it work?" and started asking "should it exist — and does it actually delight the person using it?"

That shift drove me to build case studies for Swiggy, Rapido, Zepto, and boAt. Not for an assignment. Just because I couldn't stop seeing the gaps.

I bring Salesforce depth most PMs lack, technical fluency that earns engineering trust, and 4 years of understanding exactly how products fail.

I'm not transitioning away from QA. I'm graduating from it.

"The best PMs I know were obsessed with the product long before they had the title."

Swiggy Case Study
I've been thinking about why Swiggy hasn't built a proper Tiffin vertical yet. Working professionals want ghar ka khana every day — not restaurant food. But there's no dedicated home chef discovery, no subscription plan, no trust system built around daily home cooking. So I built the product spec for it: → A 'Tiffin' vertical with local home chef onboarding → Cuisine + meal-type filters (no maida, Jain, high-protein) → Trust ratings based on hygiene + taste reviews → Weekly subscription for daily meals The habit loop is stronger than any restaurant order. The retention is higher. The emotional brand connection is deeper. Building product case studies like this as I transition into APM roles. Would love feedback from PMs in the food/consumer space 🙏 #ProductManagement #APM #Swiggy #ProductThinking
